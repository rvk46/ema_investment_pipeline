# Amby Health — Watch
**Score: 64/100** · Promising team and product concept, but lacks traction.

**What they do:** Amby Health provides an AI-powered solution for EMS agencies to automate quality reviews and billing processes, aiming to reduce manual work and optimize revenue.

**Team:** Co-founders Yos Wagenmans and Timmy Dang bring strong technical and domain expertise, with backgrounds in AI, healthcare, and software development from institutions like MIT CSAIL, Meta, Amazon, and Bloomberg.

**Market & why now:** The EMS sector is ripe for automation due to its regulatory nature and the inefficiencies in manual processes. The need for compliance and efficiency improvements presents a timely opportunity.

**Moat:** The product offers some defensibility through its integration with EMS processes and real-time feedback capabilities, though further evidence is needed to solidify this advantage.

**Traction:** Currently, there is no evidence of external traction or customer adoption, and the product lacks visibility on platforms like Hacker News.
