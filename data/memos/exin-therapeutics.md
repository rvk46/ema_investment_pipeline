# Exin Therapeutics — Watch
**Score: 53/100** · Promising team and market, but lacks external traction and clarity on AI specifics

**What they do:** Exin Therapeutics is developing an AI-driven drug discovery platform focused on neurotherapeutics, targeting neurological disorders like epilepsy and Parkinson's disease.

**Team:** The team includes three Oxford-trained neuroscientists, notably Gabriel Ocana Santero and Marko Tvrdic, with strong backgrounds in neuroscience and pharmacology.

**Market & why now:** The neurotherapeutics market is significant, addressing millions affected by neurological disorders. AI offers a timely solution to accelerate drug discovery in this complex field.

**Moat:** Proprietary AI models and in-house data provide some defensibility, though details on integration and compliance remain unclear.

**Traction:** Progress in R&D and patent submissions noted, but lacks external traction signals like partnerships or customer validation.
