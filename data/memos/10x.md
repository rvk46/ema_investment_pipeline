# 10x — Watch
**Score: 65/100** · Early traction in a niche market but lacks strong differentiation and external validation.

**What they do:** 10x provides AI-driven sales teams for dental practices, focusing on converting missed revenue opportunities into booked appointments. The product integrates with practice management systems to identify overdue recalls and unscheduled treatments, using AI to contact patients via calls and texts to schedule appointments.

**Team:** The team consists of Max Bohun, Founder/CEO, and Aman Garg, Founder/CTO. Max has experience in AI applications for dental practices, while Aman has a background in AI for educational tools and studied CS at Cornell. The team appears to have relevant technical expertise but lacks explicit prior-exit signals.

**Market & why now:** The product targets dental practices, a segment within the healthcare industry, aiming to increase revenue by optimizing patient recall processes. The market opportunity lies in the inefficiencies of current patient follow-up systems, with a focus on practices that already have patient databases.

**Moat:** Integration with practice management systems and HIPAA compliance provide some defensibility, but the product could be replicated by competitors.

**Traction:** The product is live in multiple dental groups across the U.S., showing early traction, but lacks external validation from sources like Hacker News.
