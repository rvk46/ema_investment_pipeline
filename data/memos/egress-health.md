**What they do:** Egress Health offers AI agents that automate revenue cycle management tasks for dental offices, such as eligibility checks, claims scrubbing, payment posting, and denial management.

**Moat:** Integration with insurance websites and practice management systems provides some defensibility, though details are sparse.
