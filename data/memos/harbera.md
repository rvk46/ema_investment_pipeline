# Harbera — Watch
**Score: 72/100** · Promising team and market fit, but lacks traction evidence.

**What they do:** Harbera provides AI-driven software for healthcare provider credentialing, automating monitoring and re-credentialing processes to ensure providers remain in-network with insurers.

**Team:** Sophia Clark and Veronica Nutting, both with strong technical backgrounds from Harvard and experience in healthcare and tech companies like House Rx and Stripe.

**Market & why now:** The healthcare credentialing market is ripe for automation due to its complexity and the necessity of maintaining compliance with insurance requirements.

**Moat:** Integration and automation features offer some defensibility, but proprietary technology details are unclear.

**Traction:** No evidence of customer adoption or partnerships is currently available.
