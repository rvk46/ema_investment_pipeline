# Uncommon Therapeutics — Watch
**Score: 50/100** · Promising team and market, but lacks alignment with fund's thesis

**What they do:** Uncommon Therapeutics is developing therapeutics for rare diseases, starting with Rett Syndrome, using a polytherapy strategy that leverages existing technologies and deep disease expertise.

**Team:** The team is led by Noah Auerhahn, a serial entrepreneur with personal motivation in rare disease therapies, and Ryan Lim, a biotech entrepreneur and biomedical scientist specializing in neurological disorders. Their backgrounds provide a strong domain fit for the biotech industry.

**Market & why now:** The biotech market, particularly in rare diseases, is ripe for innovation due to advancements in AI and the expiration of key biotech patents, which lower the barriers for developing new drugs.

**Moat:** The company's deep disease expertise and unique polytherapy approach offer some defensibility, although the biotech sector remains inherently high-risk.

**Traction:** There is limited external traction, with no significant presence on platforms like Hacker News and no open roles, indicating early-stage development.
