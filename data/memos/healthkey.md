# HealthKey — Watch
**Score: 70/100** · Promising AI-driven solution in healthcare with integration challenges.

**What they do:** HealthKey uses AI to prescreen patients for clinical trials by integrating with EHR systems, aiming to streamline patient enrollment and increase clinic revenue.

**Team:** Josh Sabol, Founder/CEO, has a background in automation and B2B product management from AWS and Plate IQ. Additional team details are lacking.

**Market & why now:** The healthcare industry is increasingly adopting AI solutions, and HealthKey addresses a clear pain point in clinical trial enrollment, offering financial incentives for clinics.

**Moat:** Integration with EHR systems and AI-driven prescreening provide some defensibility, but proprietary technology details are limited.

**Traction:** Early-stage with no significant external traction signals, though the company has launched and is actively seeking customers.
