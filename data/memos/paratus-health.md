# Paratus Health — Watch
**Score: 68/100** · Promising AI application in healthcare, but lacks external validation and integration details.

**What they do:** Paratus Health offers AI-powered voice agents for outpatient facilities to automate patient intake, aiming to replace traditional forms and triage nurses with conversational AI.

**Team:** The founders, Pablo Bermudez-Canete and Tannen Hall, have strong technical expertise in digital healthcare and AI from Stanford, but lack prior entrepreneurial experience or exits.

**Market & why now:** The product addresses inefficiencies in outpatient healthcare facilities, with a growing market for healthcare IT solutions driven by the need for cost reduction and efficiency improvements.

**Moat:** The use of Schmitt-Thompson protocols and claimed integration capabilities suggest some defensibility, though details are sparse.

**Traction:** Claims of being trusted by hundreds of companies and positive patient interaction metrics are noted, but lack external validation or named customer references.
