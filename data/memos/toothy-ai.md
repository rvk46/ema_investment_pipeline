# Toothy AI — Take a meeting
**Score: 78/100** · Strong team and market fit in a ripe industry for disruption

**What they do:** Toothy AI develops AI agents to automate insurance verification and billing processes for dental clinics, aiming to significantly reduce administrative burdens.

**Team:** The founding team includes Johnny Chen and Matt Kerrigan, both second-time founders with AI and automation experience, and Tejas Konduru, who focuses on healthcare AI.

**Market & why now:** The dental industry is burdened with costly and time-consuming back-office operations. With labor shortages and financial pressures, there's a strong demand for automation solutions like Toothy AI.

**Moat:** The product integrates with dental practice workflows and complies with HIPAA, providing some defensibility.

**Traction:** Early customer adoption is indicated, but there is no significant external traction or presence on platforms like Hacker News yet.
